CertChain

CertChain is a blockchain-based certificate verification platform built to confirm the authenticity of academic and professional certificates. It stores certificate hashes on-chain, allowing anyone to verify a document's validity without relying on a central authority.

Tech Stack

  • Backend: Django, Django REST Framework
  • Database: PostgreSQL
  • Blockchain: Ethereum, Solidity, Ganache, Web3.py
  • Integrity: SHA-256 hashing
  • Infrastructure: Docker
